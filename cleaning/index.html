Home Maintenance Services

The full upkeep of a home under one roof — cleaning, repairs, gardens, painting, small-space design, and moves. One studio, one standard, and one person accountable for all of it.

01

House Cleaning

Exacting, dependable cleaning on a schedule that suits your household — recurring, seasonal, or on demand. Deep and seasonal cleans, move-in and move-out, for condos, homes, and pieds-à-terre.

02

Minor Household Repairs

Furniture repairs, upholstery, and other small household fixes — including deck repair and patching. The little jobs that never quite get done, finally handled.

03

Garden Maintenance

Planting and pruning, seasonal care, and fall and spring clean-ups that keep your outdoor space as considered as the rooms inside.

04

Small-Space Solutions

Creative answers for compact living — whether custom shelving or clever use of what you already own. Save space, save money.

05

Interior Painting

Guidance on colour schemes, careful preparation, and clean painting — from a single accent wall to a full refresh.

06

Relocation & Moves

Moving logistics, packing, and unpacking — managed end to end so that a move feels effortless.

Estate Management

Composed, confidential handling of the estate and downsizing — organized from the first sort to the final sale. Handled quietly, respectfully, and without adding to a difficult time.

01

Sorting & Cataloguing

A careful, respectful first sort of a lifetime's belongings — catalogued and organized so that nothing of value or meaning is overlooked.

02

Staging & Valuation

Thoughtful presentation and fair valuation of items, so the estate is shown at its best and priced with care.

03

Estate Sale Management

The sale itself, managed from the first sort to the final sale — set-up, pricing, oversight, and wind-down.

04

Downsizing & Clear-Outs

Measured downsizing and full clear-outs, handled with discretion and an eye for what deserves to be kept.

05

Discretion Throughout

Every estate is handled quietly and in confidence, with respect for the family and the moment.
