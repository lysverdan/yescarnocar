What the Service is — and what it is not

Yes Car No Car helps you understand a used vehicle before you buy it or while you own it. You give us a vehicle identifier — typically a VIN, scanned or typed — along with details about the vehicle and your situation. We return a plain-language assessment. Every assessment resolves to one of four verdicts:

  • Good Car
  • Good Car — With Conditions
  • Good / Bad Car For You
  • Bad Car For You

A free verdict is delivered at the model level — it describes the year, make, model, and engine generally. A paid report is specific to the individual vehicle: it draws on paid third-party vehicle history data, current market research, and the details you supply, and it includes a limited number of follow-up questions about that report.

Our verdicts are opinions

Every verdict and every report is our informed opinion, based on the data available to us at the moment it is produced. It is not a statement of fact about your vehicle, and it is not:

  • a mechanical inspection, or a substitute for one;
  • an appraisal, or a guarantee of a vehicle’s value, condition, safety, or history;
  • a warranty, insurance, or a promise about how a vehicle will perform;
  • legal, financial, tax, or other professional advice.

You alone are responsible for your decisions about buying, selling, keeping, insuring, or repairing any vehicle. We recommend an in-person inspection by a qualified mechanic before any purchase. Always verify open recalls directly with the manufacturer or the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) using the vehicle’s VIN.
